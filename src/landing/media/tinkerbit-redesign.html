ROOM TO GROW · BLOCKS → IoT → EDGE AI

From a blinking LED to a board that thinks.

The ESP32 inside has Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and enough power for tiny AI models. Kids level up without ever changing boards.

LEVEL 01 / CIRCUITS

Blink, beep, sense

Lights, buttons, buzzers and sensors. The first wins that make kids want to keep going.

LEVEL 02 / IoT

Connect it to the internet

Send sensor readings to a phone, switch things on from anywhere, build a weather station or a plant that texts you.

LEVEL 03 / EDGE AI

Teach it to recognise things

Train a tiny AI model to spot claps, voice words or gestures, and run it right on the board. No cloud needed.
